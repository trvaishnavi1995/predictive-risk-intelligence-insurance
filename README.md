# Workers' Compensation - Claims Intelligence & Risk Analytics Using Python
## Project Overview

This is an insurance analytics project focused on analyzing Workers' Compensation claims data to uncover workforce risk patterns, injury trends, industry exposure, and geographic claim concentrations.

Using Python for data preprocessing and Power BI for visualization, this project transforms raw insurance claims data into actionable business intelligence that supports risk management, underwriting decisions, and workplace safety initiatives.

---

## Business Problem

Workers' Compensation insurers and employers face challenges in identifying high-risk industries, workforce segments, and geographic hotspots that contribute to increased claim frequency and severity.

The objective of this project is to:

* Analyze historical claims data
* Identify workforce injury patterns
* Evaluate industry risk exposure
* Detect geographic claim hotspots
* Forecast future claims trends
* Recommend risk mitigation strategies

## Dataset Filtering

The original Workers’ Compensation claims dataset contained records beginning from the year 2000. 

For this project, the dataset was filtered to include claims data from 2010 to 2026 in order to:
- Focus on modern insurance claim trends
- Improve consistency in reporting formats
- Reduce outdated or incomplete historical records
- Enhance the relevance of predictive risk analysis
- Improve computational efficiency for large-scale exploratory data analysis
- Claim types and policy-related variables

## Key Insights

### Workforce Risk

Employees aged 26–55 account for the majority of workplace injury claims, representing the highest-risk workforce segment.

### Gender Exposure

Male workers contribute approximately 57% of total claims, indicating higher exposure in physically demanding occupations.

### Injury Trends

Non-Compensable and Temporary claims represent the largest proportion of claims across the dataset.

### Geographic Hotspots

Several counties consistently report elevated claim volumes, highlighting geographic areas requiring focused safety interventions.

### Industry Risk

Healthcare, Public Administration, Transportation, and Manufacturing industries demonstrate the highest operational risk exposure.
